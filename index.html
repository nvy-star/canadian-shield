05 — Performance Glazing specification

BUILT FOR
AN ONTARIO
WINTER.

i

Argon between the panes

Argon is denser than air, which slows the transfer of heat through the window. The result is a warmer interior glass surface and noticeably less draft along the sill.

ii

UV protection on the glass

Blocks the wavelengths responsible for fading hardwood, rugs and upholstery, without reducing the light entering the room.

iii

Energy Star rated

Certified for Canadian climate zones rather than a general efficiency claim.

iv

Canadian made

Every unit is manufactured locally in Hamilton, Ontario. Nothing imported, nothing white-labelled.
